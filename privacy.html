Drive Uploader

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Drive Uploader is a personal command-line tool. Its owner runs it on their own computer to upload files to their own Google Drive. It is not offered as a service to the public.

What Google data it accesses

Drive Uploader uses the Google Drive API with one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With that permission it can see and change only the files and folders it has created itself. It cannot read, list, change or delete anything else in your Google Drive.

Within its own files, it reads names, folder locations and checksums. It uses them to put uploads in the right folder and to skip files that are already there.

Where data is stored

Drive Uploader has no server. The sign-in token Google issues is saved only on the computer where the tool runs. Files go directly from that computer to Google Drive, and nothing is sent anywhere else.

Sharing

Drive Uploader does not share, sell or transfer any data. It has no analytics, tracking or advertising, and no person other than the account owner has access to anything it handles.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Drive Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke Drive Uploader's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Deleting the tool's saved token file removes the sign-in from your computer. Files it uploaded stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
